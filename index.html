Physician-scientist in training working at the intersection of medical physics, computational imaging, Monte Carlo modeling, radiation biology, and human spaceflight.

About

I am currently pursuing an MD at Indiana University after prior training in medical physics at Duke and physics at Oxford. My work has centered on radiation science, computational imaging, Monte Carlo simulation, and the use of physics-based methods to answer clinically and operationally meaningful questions.

Selected Work

Physics across scales

Artemis I / AstroRad Radiation Protection

Led scientific evaluation of the AstroRad vest during NASA's Artemis I mission, helping assess wearable shielding for deep-space radiation protection.

Astronaut Radiation Exposure Simulation

Developed general-purpose computational models for evaluating astronaut radiation exposure at arbitrary locations in the solar system using digital phantoms and Monte Carlo methods.

Physics-Informed Neutron CT Reconstruction

Contributed to constrained optimization methods for mechanical strain tensor reconstruction from hyperspectral neutron computed tomography data.
