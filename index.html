Research & Experience

From detector physics to human spaceflight

2026–Present

Research Assistant · Purdue University

Developing physics-informed constrained optimization methods for mechanical strain tensor reconstruction from hyperspectral neutron computed tomography data in collaboration with Oak Ridge National Laboratory.

2022–2024

Lead Scientist, Space Exploration · StemRad

Led scientific evaluation of the AstroRad radiation-protection vest in deep space during NASA's Artemis I mission, contributed to astronaut human-factors testing aboard the ISS, and managed a collaboration on recycling plastic waste into 3D-printable radiation-shielding material.

2021–2022

Computational Scientist · Oak Ridge National Laboratory

Developed a GEANT4-based framework for embedding microscopic multicellular models in voxelized digital human phantoms and modeling fractionated irradiation and tumor cell proliferation over time.

2019–2021

Graduate Student Researcher · Duke RAI Labs

Built computational models for predicting human radiation exposure during space exploration missions at arbitrary locations in the solar system.

2018–2019

Postbaccalaureate Research Fellow · UC San Diego

Developed convolutional neural networks to segment brain metastases in T1 MRI and track lesions longitudinally in stereotactic radiosurgery patients.
