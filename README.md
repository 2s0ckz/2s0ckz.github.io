Version 36 makes proton multiple-scatter secondaries genuinely sparse.

New behavior:
- only 35% of protons are eligible to emit a multiple-scatter secondary
- eligible protons emit at a randomly scheduled scatter vertex between 7 and 14
- maximum one multiple-scatter secondary per proton lifetime
- daughter type: ~85% electron / ~15% photon
- scatter-produced electron initial segment: 24–50 px
- proton always continues after the emission
- terminal proton interactions remain separate and unchanged
